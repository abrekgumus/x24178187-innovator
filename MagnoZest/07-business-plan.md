# Business Plan: MagnoZest (Karakum Haptics Ltd.)

## 1. Executive Summary
MagnoZest is a revolutionary haptic data interface designed for extreme environmental surveying. By converting complex magnetic subterranean data into physical resistance and employing olfactory stress-reduction, we solve the "sensory drift" problem for mineral explorers in the Karakum Desert.

## 2. Market Opportunity
*   **Total Addressable Market (TAM):** $2.5B global mineral exploration equipment market.
*   **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):** $150M focused on Central Asian startups and independent survey firms.
*   **Growth Driver:** The global push for rare-earth minerals and the digitalization of the Silk Road economic belt.

## 3. Product Roadmap
*   **Q4 2026:** Beta testing of "The Kurban" V1 prototype in Turkmenistan.
*   **Q2 2027:** Full commercial launch of MagnoZest V1.
*   **Q4 2027:** Enterprise API integration for real-time cloud mapping.

## 4. Marketing Campaign: "The Pulse of the Karakum"
### A. Vision
Position MagnoZest as the "sixth sense" for the modern explorer.

### B. Channels
*   **Deep Tech Trade Shows:** Presence at PDAC (Toronto) and Mining Indaba (Cape Town).
*   **Targeted LinkedIn Campaign:** Reaching C-suite and Field Leads in junior mining companies.
*   **Documentary Series:** "Feeling the Desert" – A cinematic YouTube series showcasing field trials in Turkmenistan.

### C. Creative Assets
*   **Slogan:** "Navigate the Invisible."
*   **Imagery:** High-contrast photos of the Karakum dunes juxtaposed with sleek, titanium haptic devices.
*   **Scent-Based DM:** High-value leads receive a physical "Zest-Aura" sample kit (lemon-scented haptic material).

## 5. Financial Projections
*   **Unit Price:** $12,500 (Hardware) + $1,200/year (SaaS Subscription).
*   **Year 1 Goal:** 200 units ($2.5M revenue).
*   **Year 3 Goal:** 1,500 units + SaaS expansion ($20M revenue).
