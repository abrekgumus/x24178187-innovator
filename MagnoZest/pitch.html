Lessons Learned

  • Constraint Breeds Innovation: Weird parameters (Koalas in deserts) force non-linear thinking.
  • Multisensory Design: Solving for sight (glare) required engaging touch and smell.
  • Context is King: A product for Turkmenistan needs unique local considerations (heat, Soviet tech history).
  • Haptics > Visuals: In high-stress environments, tactile feedback is more resilient than LCDs.
  • Stress as a Signal: Using GSR to trigger olfactory relief (Lemon) adds a wellness layer to industrial tools.
  • Storytelling as Strategy: The narrative of Kurban the Koala led directly to the haptic chess-grid interface.
